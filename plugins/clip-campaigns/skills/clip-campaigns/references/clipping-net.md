# Clipping.net — clipping.net/dashboard

Campaign list: https://clipping.net/dashboard/campaigns

These notes describe what to look for, not fixed selectors. Confirm against the live
page with `browser_snapshot`, and record what you actually find in
`campaigns/site-notes.md`.

## Reading the campaign list
- The dashboard lists campaigns as cards or rows. For each capture: name, brand,
  rate (per 1K views / CPM), budget and how much is left (progress bars are common —
  read the numbers, not the bar), allowed platforms, content category, and the link
  to its detail page (the id is in that URL).
- Look for tabs such as available vs. joined/my campaigns, and paginate or scroll to
  the end.

## Campaign detail page
Save the full rules section verbatim to `brief.md`. Look for: where the source
footage lives, required captions/hashtags/tags, banned edits, length limits,
per-clip or per-account caps, minimum view thresholds, review time, and whether an
application/join step comes first.

## Submitting
Expect a "submit clip"/"add submission" flow that takes the post URL. Some
campaigns require the social account to be connected/verified first — if so, stop
and tell the user; do not connect accounts yourself.

## Earnings
Look under the dashboard for submissions, analytics, balance or payouts. Read
statuses, view counts, rejections with reasons, and balance. Do not request payouts
or edit payment details.
