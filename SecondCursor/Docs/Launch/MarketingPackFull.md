## 1. The 15 most viral moments, ranked

### 1.0 What the 2024 to 2026 comparables show

Sources marked (search) were read as search-result summaries only, not full pages.
- **Buckshot Roulette:** spread on creator Let's Plays and short clips. Markiplier's Jan 9, 2024 video reached 2.4M views in one day, a TikTok gameplay clip reached 3.9M in six days, and ending-explainer videos reached 400k to 694k in a week ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/buckshot-roulette)). Both live reaction and "ending explained" formats scale. Expect explainers of our endings and gate our own posts.
- **KinitoPET:** the reported hooks are personal fourth-wall beats: a drawing app writing the player's name, a camera app showing their face, a location lookup. It also has a Streamer Mode, and some players worried about their real files ([Videogaming Wiki](https://videogaming.fandom.com/wiki/KinitoPET), search). "It knows me" travels. Our version is the same feeling with a guarantee that nothing real is touched.
- **The Exit 8:** TikTok is full of challenge framings such as "can't miss any anomalies" and all-anomaly runs ([example](https://www.tiktok.com/@austinfelt/video/7502093057265847583), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exit_8), search). Participation beats spectacle. Our version is "what would you type".
- **Desktop Explorer (fake OS, June 2026 Next Fest):** the demo runs about an hour, and coverage was long no-commentary playthroughs plus Polygon's best-demos list ([Venn Studios](https://venn-studios.com/research/next-fest-demos/desktop-explorer/)). Plan a 10 to 15 minute quiet-cut of Night 1 for YouTube alongside the shorts.
- **No, I'm not a Human (Sept 2025):** streams titled with the game's own phrase (RTGame, shxtou) and a supercut series ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GjLBAxPKCTg), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No,_I%27m_Not_a_Human), search). A quotable phrase in the title does the marketing. Our candidates are SO AM I, THE LAST ONE SAID THAT and STILL THERE / GOOD.
- **Mouthwashing:** TikTok volume is fan animation and memes built around characters ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/mouthwashing-meme), search). SECOND CURSOR has no faces, so the two cursors are the characters. Ship a free cursor sticker pack and an edit template.
- **Iron Lung:** the game has a one-sentence premise, and the January 2026 film grossed $51.2M on a $3M budget ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Lung_(video_game)), search). Keep ours: the scariest thing on screen is a mouse pointer that isn't yours.
- **Format data (vendor blogs, directional only):** TikTok gaming clips complete best at 15 to 34 s, Shorts tolerate 30 to 60 s, and captions lift Shorts completion 12 to 15% ([Clypse](https://clypse.ai/blog/best-tiktok-gaming-clip-length-2026), [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-caption-subtitle-best-practices)).
- **Next Fest guidance ([presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/steam-next-fest-guide)):** show gameplay in the first 5 s of a trailer. End the demo on a cliffhanger. Build the creator list 4 to 6 weeks out and email about 1 week out. Between 68% and 88% of fest wishlists come from people who never played the demo, so the trailer and page carry most of the load.

**Ranking logic:** context-free (a viewer needs zero explanation), reaction strength, chat participation, fit under 20 s and readable muted, and difference per player (every creator gets a different clip). Spoiler cost is applied after ranking, not before. Every clip is 20 s or shorter with burned-in captions of the typed lines.

### 1.2 The 15, in detail

#### 1. Wave at yourself (CAM 03)
- **Night and beat:** Night 1, `camera`. The ghost types LOOK AT YOU and opens CAM 03 itself.
- **Clip (17 s):**
  - 0 to 2 s: CAM 03 already open. REC dot, running timestamp, a seated figure at WS-04. Caption: "This camera is pointed at my chair."
  - 2 to 5 s: Nothing moves. A handcam inset shows a still hand on the mouse.
  - 5 to 8 s: The hand nudges and the figure's arm moves with it.
  - 8 to 13 s: A big wave. The figure waves. Each click is echoed by a quiet delayed click (already implemented).
  - 13 to 17 s: The streamer says "no." LOOK AT YOU is still typing underneath. Cut on the last letter.
- **Why it clips:** every player tests the camera within seconds: a pause, a quiet "no", then a wave. It needs no context and works muted.
- **Clip title:** "The security camera in this game is pointed at my chair"
- **Hook text (0 to 2 s):** "This camera is pointed at my chair"
- **Game-side change:**
  - Add a 6 px name tag above the figure reading `CROURKE / WS-04`. A viewer who missed the intro then knows it is the player.
  - The arm ignores the mouse for the first 1.2 s after the feed opens, then answers with a 0.3 s lag before tracking normally. The dead second is the beat where the streamer wiggles the mouse and nothing happens.
- **Spoiler:** S0 up to LOOK AT YOU. Never include what follows the door and doorway sequence (the head turn).

#### 2. I asked the ghost X
- **Night and beat:** Night 1, `communication` (three exchanges). It recurs in Night 2 (`ex2_back`) and Night 3 (`ex3_final`).
- **Clip (14 s), "chat" version:**
  - 0 to 2 s: Jotter open. The ghost's line WHO TOLD YOU / TO SHRED IT sits above. The streamer types "chat is watching you".
  - 2 to 4 s: Enter. The caret blinks. Nothing happens.
  - 4 to 9 s: WHO IS CHAT types one letter at a time, at 2x size.
  - 9 to 12 s: ARE THEY WATCHING TOO.
  - 12 to 14 s: The streamer looks at the chat panel. Hold.
- **Why it clips:** chat becomes a character inside the game. Every reply is short, in caps and screenshot-ready, and every creator's clip is different. It is a repeatable series: "make me" gets THE LAST ONE SAID THAT, "wtf" gets THEY LOG THAT TOO, "i love you" gets DONT SAY THAT HERE, "lol" gets KEEP LAUGHING / IT HELPS.
- **Clip title:** "I told the ghost my chat is watching. It asked if they're watching too."
- **Hook text:** "I told the ghost I'm streaming"
- **Game-side change:**
  - Add a 0.9 s think-pause (caret blinking, no key taps) before any keyword-hit reply starts. Fallback replies start after 0.4 s, so a hit feels earned.
  - Add one keyword group above the fallback: `skibidi, rizz, sigma, gyatt, ohio`, with reply THAT WORD IS NEW / I STOPPED KEEPING UP. Chat will type these in the first minute of every stream, and a deadpan answer that carries no date hint is a free clip. Place it above the "who/what" group.
- **Spoiler:** S0.
