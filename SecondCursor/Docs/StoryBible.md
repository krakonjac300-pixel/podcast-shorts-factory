# SECOND CURSOR -- Story Bible (Vertical Slice)

All names, companies and events are fictional. In-game text is printable ASCII only.

## 1. Premise

Tuesday night, November 17, 1998. At 1:52 AM Casey Rourke (employee 214) logs on for a first solo shift (2:00 to 7:00 AM) at workstation WS-04, Office B-7, on the basement B-Level of Letheworth Data Reclamation. The job is archive, verify, shred. The company says the drives hold legacy customer data. One file keeps coming back: employee_017.dat. Then a second cursor shows up and will not let Casey shred it.

Tone: corporate banality first, dread second. Every scare is an act of control or knowledge. Something moves your file, answers your question, knows your employee number, shows you the back of your own head.

## 2. The Company

Letheworth Data Reclamation ("Clean drives. Clear minds.") was founded 06/14/71 by Arthur C. Letheworth. The public business is wiping and reselling storage. The real business is Continuity. Anyone who works long enough at a NEXUS terminal leaves an impression in the machine: keystrokes, pointer paths, timing, hesitation. Letheworth captures and keeps these impressions. Clients' estates pay to keep them going after death (the "Estate of..." lines in the ledgers). When an account lapses, the impression is "reclaimed", which means a night operator shreds it believing it is a spreadsheet.

Letheworth keeps its own staff too (Policy 7.4). When an operator's impression is complete, Custodial Services "clears the seat". Retained staff go on working. Anton Voss, Director of Retention, has been dead since 1989 and still signs orders.

NEXUS OS 4.1 is made by Orrery Computing Corp. The capture service, LWCAP.EXE, belongs to Letheworth.

Writers' rule: never state any of this outright. The slice only uses the words retained, impression, persistent and seat cleared.

## 3. The Entity (hidden truth)

In canon, the second cursor is Ellen Marsh, employee 017. She was the night operator in B-7 from 1971 until 03/02/87 at 02:17 AM, when she became the first operator retained on WS-04. Her retention went wrong because she never went quiet. For eleven years her session has stayed open, and she has watched every operator who sat in her chair: Lundy (cleared 1993), Achterberg (1997) and Pruitt (11/02/98). She fights reclamation because no new impression on WS-04 can finish while 017 holds the seat. Deleting her file doesn't only delete her. It also frees the seat for Casey.

She means to protect Casey, but she isn't safe to be around. Eleven years inside the machine have left her with only the machine's tools: watching, recording and keeping. She recorded Casey to prove that the company is recording Casey. When the Custodian comes, the only rescue she knows is the company's own method, so she keeps a copy.

**The slice reveals:** the cursor is intelligent, resists deletion, knows Casey's number, can override locked apps, has recorded Casey, is afraid of the camera and points Casey to 017's record.

**The slice withholds:** that the cursor is Ellen (her name never appears in its lines, and asked for a name it says "THEY TOOK THAT FIRST"). It also withholds what retention is, what the Custodian is, whether Casey survived, and whether the voice that types "SEE YOU TOMORROW NIGHT" is a rescuer or a replacement.

Voice: ALL CAPS, 1-6 words, no apostrophes, never explains. It types like someone with little time and little of themselves left. Sometimes warm ("STAY WITH ME"), sometimes chilling ("SO AM I").

## 4. Employee 017 -- Ellen R. Marsh

Hired 06/21/71. Status: DECEASED. Office: B-7 (WS-04). Last login: 11/18/98 1:52 AM, the same minute Casey logged on. Supervisor: R. Hale. Ruth filed the 1987 incident report and left the comment field blank. The notes end with "Replacement assigned: 214 (C. Rourke)."

Her timesheet shows 8.0 hours every night since 1987, with 0 shifts missed. Her .dat payload is broken first-person fragments: the heater, the door, losing her name, "dont let them finish you", "i will keep the next one".

## 5. The Figure on Camera

The company calls it Custodial Services (Personnel 000, hired 06/14/71, "Does not converse"). Nobody ever calls it a monster. It is tall and still, with its head bowed and a gray coverall, and its face never comes through the camera noise.

Its rule is never stated. The entity only implies it with "IT MOVES WHEN YOU WATCH": the figure advances only while someone watches it through Letheworth's cameras. Each time Casey reopens CAM 03 it is closer, and it is never seen moving. Earlier emails set this up: "remain seated", "do not turn around", "do not stand in doorways". Custodial rounds start at 3:00 AM, and that is when seats get cleared. The dead director's shred order arrives at 2:17, Ellen's minute. Tonight Ruth warns that Custodial is coming down early.

## 6. The Previous Night Operator -- Gary Pruitt (209)

Hired 02/16/98. Funny, practical and a little lonely (vending-machine tips, a mug with ducks on it). His notes go from mundane to strange. He writes that the cursor pulled him away from the Camera Viewer "like a hand on your wrist", that it typed NOT YET, and that "I think I had it backwards." The last entry, 11/02/98 at 2:50 AM, stops mid-word: "don't fi" (fight? finish?).

Personnel lists him as ON LEAVE, with personal effects held by Custodial. His car sat in the lot for a week. The Retention queue lists him as HELD - INCOMPLETE: he was collected, but his impression could not finish because 017 held the seat. Whether that was a mercy or a trap is a question for a later night.

## 7. Key Mysteries (left open)

1. Who controls the second cursor? It could be 017, Gary, something wearing both, or Casey's own recorded input.
2. Why is it here, and why tonight?
3. Is it hostile, or is it protecting me?
4. What is Letheworth really doing with the drives, and with its staff?
5. What happened to Gary?
6. What is Custodial, and why does it only move when watched?

## 8. Clues That Point Both Ways

| Clue | Malicious reading | Protective reading |

| Fights the shred, drags the file back | Self-preservation | Deleting 017 frees the seat for Casey |
| Shred order signed by A. Voss | The company is removing a threat | The order comes from a dead man |
| Records and replays Casey's input; "SO AM I" | It is learning Casey in order to replace them | Proof that the company records Casey |
| Unlocks CAM 03 | Watching is what brings Custodial | Casey had to see the danger |
| Kept Gary from the camera, shows Casey | Inconsistent and manipulative | It learned that not looking did not save Gary |
| "HE DIDNT WANT TO SEE" vs Gary's notes | It lies about Gary | Gary misread it |
| "Replacement assigned: 214" | It wants a successor so it can leave | It is fighting the assignment |
| "I KEPT A COPY OF YOU" | It did to Casey what Letheworth does | It saved what it could |

## 9. Text Map by Beat

- **Boot:** the BIOS lists Pointing Device 2 and "Previous session was not closed."
- **Work:** the briefing teaches the rules. Work order 3318 asks to wipe an ACTIVE employee's drive, requested by Retention.
- **Anomaly:** IT blames the pointer driver and says to "let it finish".
- **Presence / Conflict:** the URGENT order arrives, then "in use by another user on this workstation". Ruth writes: "It did it to Gary too."
- **Communication:** three Notepad exchanges (STOP / WHO TOLD YOU / DO YOU WANT TO SEE).
- **Escalation:** the replay, then an email from Casey's own account dated 03/02/87 02:17 that says "remain seated".
- **Reveal:** CAM 03, the panic, "LOOK UP 017".
- **End:** STILL THERE / GOOD / I KEPT A COPY / OF YOU / SEE YOU TOMORROW NIGHT.

## 10. Future Nights and Endings

- **Night 1:** this slice. Casey is collected, copied, or both.
- **Night 2:** Casey logs on again, and nobody can say whether this is Casey or Ellen's copy. The door log says B-7 was never opened.
- **Night 3:** CAM 04 flickers on. Sublevel C is full of shelves of drives, each labeled with a name.
- **Night 4:** Gary surfaces inside a batch file, HELD and incomplete, begging to be finished.
- **Night 5:** Ruth Hale breaks. She calls the desk phone and writes the 1987 comment at last.
- **Night 6:** Casey learns to move a second cursor. Someone new sits at WS-04.
- **Night 7:** the sealed Chairman's office, Voss, and the last shred.

Endings:
- **SHRED:** free Ellen and take her seat.
- **KEEP:** stay retained together, working nights forever.
- **LOG OFF:** walk out at 7:00 AM, but the log shows nobody left.
- **WATCH:** turn Letheworth's cameras on Letheworth.

Notes for code: timestamps match the GameRoot clock, which starts at 1:52 AM, so rounds at 3:00 AM land near the end of the slice. Dynamic emails are stamped 2:01, 2:17 and 2:21 AM.
